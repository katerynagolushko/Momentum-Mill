The Fundraising Sprint

Investors are attracted to shiny things. Be that thing.

Fundraising is a sales process. The longer you suck at selling your vision the likelier your startup is gonna die.

The thesis

Before the raise, become someone investors will be obsessed with. Then announce your raise.

We will take you through 8 weeks of building your personal brand as the founder and making you investor-ready. This is our new methodology and we will take the cohort of up to 5 founders starting July 2026 on a heavily discounted rate to test the thesis and get the data from you.

The timeline

8 weeks of brand and prep, then a 2-week raise.

8 weeks of brand + prep, then a focused 2-week raise.

What we offer

1

Strategy Ideation

Here we analyze your startup and your background as the founder(s). What you built before, who is in your network, what stage is your startup at, etc. Based on this we will create a personal branding plan with an itinerary of activities. These will include the mix of:

2

Regular content

Regular X, LinkedIn and Substack content in your style of speech on the trending topics, optimised for your niche and virality language of the platform.

3

Events

List of events where you need to show up and enrich your network, specifically among investors and introducers.

4

First Artifact

  • Your one big content piece, unique contribution to your field. It will be posted and popularized by your network. Within 48h you've got as many relevant people in your network as possible engaging with your main content piece.
5

Salon

This is where you are supposed to be at the center IRL. Based on your budget and the preference we will pick between:

5.1 Dinner

Organizing an intimate dinner event with semi-warm investors you've met, industry stars (researchers, scientists, etc) and relevant founders. You don't pitch at the event, you don't mention the raise, you center it around the topic. You make them feel part of the unique conversation about the topic they are passionate about. If they ask if you are raising, you say no, shall I come back to you once I announce my raise?

5.2 Panel Speaker

You speak at the panel at the event that is relevant to your niche, you invite those who are relevant but this is mainly for the post event marketing. We will find relevant events and get you the spot on the panel. We will use the pictures and the videos to promote you on your social media.

5.3 Podcasts

Appearances on relevant podcasts. Finding the podcasts and reaching out to them and getting you a spot.

6

Fundraising prep

  • Getting everything ready like deck, one-pager, market, competition research, your pitch itself in multiple versions depending on the audience.
  • Documenting the investor pipeline. Every warm investor you know, your connection to them, context layer.
7

Sprint Campaign

  • Part 1: 14-10 days before the raise but at least 1 week after Salon 1 we announce it to Tier 1 Investors (your biggest priority) on email, LI or X, wherever you are connected. Give them exclusive access to your calendar booking.
  • Part 2: 7 days before the raise we send non targeted emails to every investor that is warm and whose thesis fits. We show them that your calendar is already almost full and a lot of meetings are booked, by creating a custom meeting booking UI.
