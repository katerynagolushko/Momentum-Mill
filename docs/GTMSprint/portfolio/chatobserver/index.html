The company

Chatobserver puts SEO, paid ads, content, reporting and AI visibility in one workspace for a marketing team. Alex Berish is the founder.

The week

Before the sprint, his earlier product had 2 paying customers and about £503 a month. The new platform had no customer meetings and no pipeline.

He ran two experiments, one aimed at small paid media agencies and one at smaller brands paying for several marketing tools. He contacted about 50 agency people on LinkedIn, including 26 in Norway, sent 25 emails, and pulled 496 people into one research list. A few days in, he narrowed that list to smaller agencies and rewrote the messages in his own voice. Peter Vogopoulos, his mentor, pushed him to show how much of the product was already built.

Of about 50 LinkedIn requests, 7 people connected or replied. He held 10 meetings. Five companies signed up or committed, and another agency was ready to test. One new signup at $279 a month was logged on day five, and 41 LinkedIn connections were approved during the week.
