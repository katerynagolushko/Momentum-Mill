luminocare.co.uk

The company

Luminocare builds AI agents for home care agencies. Four of them cover scheduling, CQC evidence, client concern and staff retention, and a person still confirms anything that changes a rota or a client record.

Furqhan Faisal, the CEO, is a doctor who spent ten years putting electronic patient records into the NHS.

The week

He came in with 417 discovery calls already done, and £78,000 in signed pre-purchase orders from three agencies. Those orders had not been paid, and there were no live pilots and no meetings booked.

The experiment was written down first, with a line for what would count as a fail: a personalised email, a call the next day, and a follow-up, sent to owners and registered managers in Nottinghamshire, Derbyshire and, later, Northamptonshire. 210 emails brought 4 replies, a 1.9% rate, under the 3% line he had set. On day four the phone became the main channel.

Across 79 calls he had 11 conversations with the people who decide, and 4 of those moved to a next step. Three of the calls ran past 30 minutes, and the longest ran 45 to 60 minutes. One agency agreed to come on as an early adopter and confirmed that by email. The week also produced a new purchase order worth £20,000.

Luke Balabanovic was his mentor. The advice he acted on was to make more cold calls, and to get direct numbers for the people who decide.

Luminocare won Existing Ventures at Demo Day.
