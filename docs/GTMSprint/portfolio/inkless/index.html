The company

Inkless is a private canvas where people and their AI agents design together. Fouwaz Parkar is a co-founder. The product was three weeks old when the sprint started.

The week

They had 2 users, no revenue, no pilots and 39 prompts on the product. There was no demo pipeline and no customer conversations.

The main experiment was two free game nights on Inkless itself, invited from people they already knew, then one-to-one live demos and a recorded customer interview. Cold outreach through Needle and LinkedIn got no replies. Samuel Klacman mentored the team, and those sessions settled the order of work: warm outreach before cold.

In eight days the product went from 39 prompts to 483. Both guests at the first game night started using it, and both people who sat a live demo started the same day. The team held 5 calls, including those demos, and recorded 48.1 hours of hands-on use. They have a plan to run the game night every week.
