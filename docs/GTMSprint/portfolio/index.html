gs GTM Sprint

Portfolio

8–15 Nov 2026 · London

Thirty early-stage startups spent the week of 8–15 Nov 2026 in London doing one job: getting customers. Between them they ran 45 week-long sales experiments with GTM mentors, and seven of those teams pitched at Demo Day at Encode Hub.

  • Winner, Existing Ventures

    Luminocare

    AI agents for home care agencies, covering scheduling, CQC evidence, client concern and staff retention.

    210 emails replied at 1.9%, so the week moved to the phone: 79 cold calls, a £20k purchase order, and 4 conversations still open.
  • Winner, Emerging Ventures

    CounterLabs

    Turns a shop's existing CCTV into a model of how people move and buy, without identifying them.

    Came out of stealth, walked into more than 30 shops, spoke with 20 store managers, and landed a chat with the CTO of Sainsbury's.

  • Pillr

    Marketing for UK accountants and law firms, checked against the rules before it goes out.

    147 cold calls in the week. Paying customers went from 3 to 6.

  • Chatobserver

    A marketing workspace for SEO, ads, content and AI visibility.

    Entered the week at about £503 a month from an earlier product, then held 10 meetings and logged a $279 signup.

  • Dori

    Synthetic customers a team can test with before anything ships.

    30 personal conversations led to 4 pilots. Product Hunt and Reddit brought one customer at $199.

  • TrainerFlow

    An operating system for personal trainers, from the programme to the payment.

    About 130 first messages brought 21 replies. Five coaches asked to try it. No payments by the end of the week.
  • Inkless

    Inkless

    A private canvas where a person and their AI agents design on the same surface.

    Prompts on the product went from 39 to 483. Cold messages got no replies. Live demos converted the same day.
