The company

Dori builds synthetic customers from a company's own customers, so product, marketing and research teams can test a reaction before they ship. Samuel Amin is co-founder and CEO.

The week

He started with 3 customers, £59 in revenue and no pilots.

Two experiments ran in the same week. The first went out to every warm contact he had, more than 3,000 people across LinkedIn and his own network, asking for referrals and pilots. The second was a Product Hunt and Reddit launch, plus cold LinkedIn with an offer of a free demo. He sent more than 60 LinkedIn messages and more than 100 connection requests, and he contacted more than 30 people directly. Misky Sharif and Victor Delcambre were his mentors.

The referrals did the work. Thirty personal conversations led to the pilots, while the cold outreach produced free demo calls. By the end of the week Dori had 4 pilots and 4 customers, with about £35,000 in contract and proposal value tied to those pilots. The Product Hunt and Reddit launch brought one Ultra customer at $199.
