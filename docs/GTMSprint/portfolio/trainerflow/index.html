TrainerFlow

An operating system for personal trainers, from the programme to the payment.

thetrainerflow.com

The company

TrainerFlow is an operating system for personal trainers. The programme, the record of what happens between sessions, and the payments are meant to live in one place. Filippo Ceccagno is the founder.

The week

Before the sprint, about 25 trainers and 50 clients had free access. There were no paid pilots.

He ran six experiments: LinkedIn for UK trainers, Instagram for UK coaches, coaches who already use Trainerize, and three ways of opening an Instagram message, which were a pitch first, a discovery question first, and a question about the app they use now. He sent about 130 personalised first messages. Victor Delcambre, Samuel Klacman and Peter Vogopoulos were his mentors.

He got 21 replies, about 16% overall. Among trainers who connected on LinkedIn, 32% replied, against about 12% on Instagram. Discovery-first messages reached about 15%, against 10% for a pitch first, and those conversations ran longer. Five coaches asked to see TrainerFlow or try it. He sent three personalised Loom walkthroughs, and one call is booked. By the end of the sprint there were no live meetings, no pilots and no payments.

Peter's advice that week was to charge from the first day, because giving the product away hides who actually wants it. Filippo replaced the free offer with founding-coach pricing.
