Press

GTM Sprint is a free GTM acceleration programme for technical B2B founders, run by Kateryna Golushko and Momentum Mill. From 8 to 15 November 2026 in London, 30 startups ran 45 experiments, and Demo Day was on Sunday 15 November. Founders competed on who made the most progress in their customer acquisition journey.
