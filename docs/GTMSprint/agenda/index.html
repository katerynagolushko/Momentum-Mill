Agenda

One prep day in the room. Five days selling. One day to show what happened.

Day 1

This is a sprint prep day, you will do:

  • Work with a GTM Leader
  • Figure out your ICP and USP
  • Create a GTM experiment
  • Get a qualified lead list
  • Pitch to your mentor TWICE

Founders do the work. GTM people float and review. Short tools enablement so you can build the list, not a workshop.

Days 2 to 6

Main Challenge, here is what you will do:

  • Optionally from our office in central London, or anywhere you want
  • Film your progress
  • Log your progress into our platform
  • Book a call with the mentor of your choosing
  • On Day 6: final submission (you can submit earlier)

Day 7

Closing day, here is what happens:

  • Workshop on closing while judges judge
  • Top 5 selected to pitch
  • They pitch
  • Fireside chat or panel with top GTM people
  • Winners announced
