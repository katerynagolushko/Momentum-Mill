FAQ

What was GTM Sprint?
A free, one-week sales sprint in London (8–15 Nov 2026) where 30 early-stage technical founders stopped building and went out to sell.
What made it different?
No talks, no theory. Every founder ran live sales experiments daily, logged proof every night, and worked 1:1 with operators from top startups. Free, no equity.
What did it achieve?
45 experiments in 7 days, a £20k purchase order, a £16k contract signed, £35k in contract value and 4 pilots. See the 7 finalists.
Who was involved?
14 mentors and judges from leading startups and funds, plus partners including lemlist, Apify, Needle, EWOR and Clean. Demo Day featured a fireside with George Bevis, founder of Tide.
Who ran it?
Kateryna Golushko, founder of Momentum Mill, who previously built the accelerator programme at Ultra.vc.
What's next?
More sprints in London and other cities. Partners and sponsors can get in touch.
