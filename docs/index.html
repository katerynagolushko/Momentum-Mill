GTM Sprint, cohort one

The same sprint, run as a program with 20 to 50 early-stage founders in London at the same time. You have a product people can start using and you have not figured out which channel sells it. That is the whole entry requirement. Still work in progress, but this is the shape:

Program

One month. 2 to 4 experiments per founder, weekly check-in with your GTM operator on what worked and what didn't.

Tool

Sets the ICP, the channel and the success number for each experiment. Trained on 150+ real early-stage cases, and every cohort feeds it more.

Community

Same cohort, daily in person or online, plus IRL events in London every week or two. You get a buddy matched on your case and weekly accountability.

Credits

Credits to run the experiments on the tools you would pay for anyway, Clay and Attio type of thing. List in progress.

Mentors

10 to 20 slots a week for 1:1s with GTM people who have done it. First booked, first served.

Selection

Cohort one gets the best conditions I will ever offer. In return you commit to executing, or you are out. Investors get invited in to see the cohort.
