# Product

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
Founders and lean GTM teams who cannot staff a 24/7 social desk. They open Vigil to see what their brand crew of Claude agents found, drafted, and posted across X, LinkedIn, Reddit, and Instagram — then approve or let high-confidence replies ship.

Primary job: keep brand presence alive without babysitting every mention.

## Brand Personality
Alert. Operational. Trustworthy under pressure.

## Anti-references
Purple SaaS dashboards, chatbot bubbles as the hero, fake "AI sparkle" gradients, generic analytics card grids with no agent story.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Status never by color alone.
