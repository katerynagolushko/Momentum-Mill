# Momentum Mill

## Register

brand

## Users

Post-MVP founders who have a working product and need to find a repeatable route to customers. The primary audience is early-stage founders in London who value fast, practical testing and in-person founder networks.

## Product Purpose

Momentum Mill helps founders figure out go-to-market through 2 to 4 focused growth experiments in a month. Each experiment defines an ICP, channel, success metric and deadline. The winning route gets more time and budget. The site also shows Momentum Mill's ecosystem work through real founder events, introduces the upcoming IRL GTM Sprint and points visitors to hangby.me.

## Brand Personality

Direct, restless and practical. The voice sounds like an experienced founder in the room, uses plain language and earns claims with specific work.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, vague growth promises, decorative gradients, fake product mockups, stock startup photography, inflated agency copy and polished text that could belong to any company.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the offer in one screen.
2. Show the experiment method with a concrete hypothesis.
3. Prove ecosystem work with real event photography and direct event links.
4. Keep every call to action specific and easy to find.
5. Preserve Kateryna's first-person voice and cut anything that sounds templated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable contrast and responsive layouts from small phones through large desktop screens.
